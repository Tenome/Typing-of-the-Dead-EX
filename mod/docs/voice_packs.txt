Voice packs
===========

A voice pack replaces the game's voice lines and sound effects with your own files, like texture, music and
model packs. Each pack is a folder in voices\. Choose packs in todmod_config.exe ("Voice packs", first wins) or
in todmod.ini:

  [sound]
  voice_packs=my_voices,other_pack

Folder layout
-------------
A pack mirrors the game's SOUND folder. Use the same folder and file names as the original:

  voices\my_voices\VOICE\ST1\01_J.wav      replaces SOUND\VOICE\ST1\01_J.WAV
  voices\my_voices\ST1\01_J.wav            the same (the VOICE\ folder may be left out)
  voices\my_voices\SE\COMMON\GUN5_22.wav   replaces the sound effect SOUND\SE\COMMON\GUN5_22.WAV

Files missing from every pack play the game's own sound. The configurator's "Audio" tab lists every voice line
(with its subtitle) and every effect, so you can hear which file is which.

Formats
-------
WAV (PCM), OGG and FLAC. OGG and FLAC are decoded once to voices\.cache\ (delete that folder to force a new
decode). Keep voice lines in mono or stereo, 22050 or 44100 Hz.

Notes
-----
- Kids mode voices live in SOUND\VOICE\KIDS\ and are replaced the same way (VOICE\KIDS\...).
- The "Voices" and "Effects" volume sliders apply to replaced sounds too.
- todmod.log lists the first replacements it plays ("voice packs: Sound/... -> ...").
