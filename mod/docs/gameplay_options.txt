Gameplay, sound and display options
===================================

All of these are in todmod_config.exe; the todmod.ini keys are given for reference.

Extras (Settings tab, "Extras" group)
-------------------------------------
- Live WPM, accuracy and streak ([display] show_typing=1): under the score in the top right; streak = words in a
  row without a typo.
- Read finished words aloud ([fun] speak_words=1, speech_rate -10..10, speech_volume 0..100): Windows
  text-to-speech says each word you finish. Japanese words need a Japanese Windows voice.
- Typewriter gun ([fun] typewriter_gun=1): gunshots become typewriter clacks and every finished word rings the
  bell. The sounds are generated, no files needed.
- Photo mode ([fun] photo_mode=1): F12 during play freezes the picture without the HUD; F12 saves a PNG to
  photos\, Esc goes back. The waist joint of a zombie very close to the camera can show a gap in the frozen
  picture (the pause screen has the same).
- Mute when in the background ([sound] mute_in_background=1): silent while another window is in front.
- Hide the reading line ([typing] hide_reading=1): only the word itself is shown; you type without seeing the
  letters (blind typing). Single letters and drills keep their line.
- Field of view ([display] fov=25..100, 0 = the game's 41 degrees): vertical view angle. Word boxes stay on the
  zombies; cutscene letterbox bars are resized to match.

Subtitle size and position (Subtitles tab, [text] subtitle_size / subtitle_position)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Size in percent applies to lines drawn with the Windows font; position (percent from the top, empty = the game's
82) moves every subtitle line.

Achievements (Stats tab, [stats] achievements=1)
------------------------------------------------
17 achievements (First Blood, Flawless, Machine Gun Keyboard, Night Owl ...) unlock as you play and show at the
top of the screen. The Stats tab lists them with their dates (stats\achievements.tsv). Off while [debug] autotype
is on.

Practice your misses (Stats tab, "Make a practice pack from my misses")
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Collects the words you mistyped most (then the slowest) from every game in stats\ and makes the word pack
dict\practice_misses (300 words), put first in "In use". Words are as you typed them (lower case; Japanese
words in romaji).

Volume (Settings tab, "Volume" sliders, [sound] music_volume / effects_volume / voice_volume)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The game itself only switches music, effects and voices on or off. The three sliders set each from 0 to 100 %
(100 = the game's own level). Music packs, the game's own music, the Dreamcast chainsaw loop, effects and voice
lines all follow them.

Score display (Settings tab, Extras, [display] show_score=1)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Shows the running score of each player in the top right corner during play (both players when two play). It is
the word score (base points + rank points); the chapter result then adds accuracy points and rescued citizens.

Voice packs (Settings tab, [sound] voice_packs=<pack>,<pack>)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Replace any voice line or sound effect. See voices\README.txt.

Import a character model (Settings tab, Extras)
-----------------------------------------------
Pick an OBJ, glTF or GLB file, a pack name and the character it replaces (Player 1 by default). The configurator
runs tools\obj_to_character.py or tools\gltf_to_character.py (Python 3 with Pillow and numpy:
"pip install pillow numpy"), puts the new pack first in Model packs and can choose that character for Player 1.
The converter's output is in todmod_import.log.

Blood colour (Settings tab, "Blood", [display] blood=<colour>)
--------------------------------------------------------------
The game has red and green blood textures and uses the green ones. "Red" uses the game's red ones (as in The House
of the Dead 2). purple, gold, blue, grey, black, white, pink, orange, cyan and yellow are the red blood tinted as it
loads; rainbow gives every blood texture its own colour; paintball changes the colour with every blood splash
(all blood on screen takes the new colour).
For blood of your own, make a texture pack with PNGs in textures\<pack>\scr_blood_red\ (when a colour other than
green is chosen) or textures\<pack>\scr_blood_green\ (green); see textures\README.txt. A pack's pictures win over
the tint.

Audio library (configurator "Audio" tab)
----------------------------------------
Every music track (with a name), voice line (with its subtitle, where the game has one) and sound effect of your
game folder. Double-click to play; Pause and Stop below the lists.

Infinite lives (Settings tab, Cheats, [cheats] infinite_lives=1)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Hits still knock you back, but you never lose a life.

Pause menu ([gameplay] pause_menu=1)
------------------------------------
Esc during play freezes the game and shows a menu (Up/Down to choose, Enter to confirm, Esc to resume):
- Resume: carry on where you were.
- Restart stage: start the current chapter again from its opening, keeping the mode you chose (Story or
  Training); lives and score start fresh.
- Exit to menu: back to the main menu (Arcade mode / Original mode / ...).
Music and looping sounds pause with the game. Not available during online play.

Mutators ("Mutators" tab, [mutators])
-------------------------------------
- Most lives (max_lives=1 or 2): you never have more lives than this. 1 = one hit and the game is over.
- Typos cost a life (typos_hurt=1): a wrong key on a word you are typing hurts like a zombie hit (at most once
  per hit pause, about 1.5 seconds).
- Word difficulty (word_level=easiest|hardest): the game normally makes words easier after you are hit and harder
  while you do well; this keeps them at the easiest or the hardest level.
- Words (words=reversed|scrambled): words from your word packs are shown reversed ("is" becomes "si"), or with
  their inner letters shuffled ("typing" becomes "tpiyng"; first and last letters stay). You type what you see.
- Letter case (letter_case=upper|lower|random): changes the case of pack words. Typing only checks case when
  "Capital letters must match" is on (Settings tab).
- Game speed (speed=50..200): the whole game runs slower or faster (150 = 90 updates per second).
Words and letter case only change your word packs (dict\), not the game's built-in word.HAB.

Randomizer weapons ("Randomizer" tab, [randomizer] weapons=1)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Thrown weapons (axes, hammers, pitchforks, maces, even the chainsaw) turn into a random weapon when they are
thrown.

More or fewer zombies ("Randomizer" tab, [randomizer] enemy_min / enemy_max)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each zombie the stage sends appears between enemy_min and enemy_max times (0-4). Extra zombies appear right
beside the original and the area waits for all of them. With 0 some zombies stay away, but one of each group
always comes. Only zombies that simply walk in or throw axes are doubled; bosses, the jumping zombies and zombies with scripted
entrances (through doors, from behind furniture) come once.

Zombies that look like people ([randomizer] human_zombies = 0, 25, 50 or 100)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
That percentage of zombie types is drawn with a civilian's body. They still move, attack and take words like
zombies. Zombies whose moves need their own body (axe throwers, the chainsaw zombie, the monkey-faced wall
jumper, the assassins) always keep their look.

Borderless window (Settings tab, [display] borderless=1)
--------------------------------------------------------
The game runs in a window without a frame that covers the whole screen, so Alt+Tab is instant. With width and
height 0 the game renders at the screen's size.

Typing statistics ("Stats" tab, [stats] enabled=1)
--------------------------------------------------
After every game todmod writes stats\:
- history.tsv: one line per game and player (words, correct keys, typos, accuracy, words per minute, most missed
  letters),
- <date>.txt: the full report including your hardest words and every word you typed,
- practice_words.txt: your most mistyped and slowest words; turn it into a word pack with
  "New pack from word list..." on the Settings tab.
Words per minute counts 5 keys as a word and only the time from the first to the last key of each word.

Replays ("Replays" tab, [replay])
---------------------------------
Every game is recorded to replays\ (record=1; the newest 20 are kept). A replay holds the keys of both players
for every frame, your save and the randomizer seed (a few kilobytes plus the save). "Watch selected replay"
starts the game with that replay: the keyboard does nothing until the replay ends, then you can play on.
A replay plays back exactly only with the same todmod version, word packs and settings; todmod.log names what
differs and logs "game differs from the recording" if the replay drifts. Online games are recorded too.
To watch from the command line: set TODMOD_REPLAY=<file in replays\> and start Tod.exe.
